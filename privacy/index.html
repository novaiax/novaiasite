Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how the personal analytics application ("the App") operated by Yann Wirtz handles data when accessing the TikTok Display API.

2. Information Collected

The App collects information only from the operator's own TikTok account (@yann.wtz), authorized via OAuth 2.0. Specifically:

  • Profile information: username, display name, follower count, video count.
  • Video information: list of published videos and their public engagement metrics (views, likes, comments, shares, saves).

The App does NOT collect any data from any TikTok user other than the App operator.

3. Use of Information

Information is used solely for personal content performance tracking and analytics by the operator. No data is shared with, sold to, or transmitted to any third party.

4. Data Storage

All data is stored locally on the operator's personal computer in encrypted token files and a local database. The App does not use external servers, cloud storage, or remote databases.

5. Data Retention

Data may be retained indefinitely for personal historical analytics. The operator may delete all stored data at any time by deleting the local storage files.

6. Third-Party Services

The App connects exclusively to the TikTok Display API. No other third-party services receive data from the App.

7. Security

OAuth tokens are stored locally and encrypted. The App is not exposed to the public internet and is accessible only on the operator's local machine.

8. User Rights

As the App operator is the only data subject, all rights under applicable data protection laws (GDPR, etc.) are exercised directly through local data management.

9. Compliance

The App complies with the TikTok Developer Privacy Policy requirements and applicable data protection laws.

10. Contact

For any inquiry related to this Privacy Policy, contact: yann@novaia.org
